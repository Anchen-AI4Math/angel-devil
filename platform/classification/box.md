We prove preservation of the guarded-box invariant after one Devil burn and any legal king reply.

Use the square of radius 100. Its corner arms extend six units along each incident side. The guard on a side has rank at most four, with rank plus one bounded by the king's distance from that side. The imported five-burn lemma initializes a guard whenever that distance is at least five.

If exactly one side is within distance five, advance that side's guard. The chosen burn lies in the square because the king is more than five units from the other sides. A positive rank decreases by one; a rank-zero guard completes the blocking triple and can be maintained after any horizontal unit displacement. The triple prevents a crossing.

If two sides are close, their corner arms already supply the blocking triples. Opposite sides cannot both be close. All other sides remain far enough to initialize a fresh guard. Thus one burn always preserves the invariant. This implements Kutz's Figure 3 with deliberately generous constants.
