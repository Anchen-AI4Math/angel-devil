Assuming the two paper milestones, the Angel of power two has a winning strategy.
The child statements are open obligations, not conclusions established by this

Suppose there were no winning power-two Angel strategy. Theorem 2.7, in the
formulation that includes the introduction's determinacy observation, supplies a
positive radius and a Nice Devil that confines every finite safe play to that box.
Theorem 4.5 supplies a single Angel strategy that survives and escapes every box
against every Nice Devil. Apply this strategy to the confining Nice Devil.

Its infinite survival makes each finite prefix safe. Its escape property supplies
a round outside the confining box, while the confinement property places that same
round inside the box. This contradiction proves the main conclusion conditional
on the two imported milestones. This is the last deduction on manuscript page 8,
after Theorem 4.5.
